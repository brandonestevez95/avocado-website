The Setup

A place you've never heard of: Michoacán

About three in four of Mexico's avocados grow in a single state, and 90% of Mexico's avocado exports come to the United States. That is a lot of guacamole resting on one place.

The price tag leaves some things out.

Water
68B L

Soil in the avocado belt lost 68 billion liters of moisture, 2001–2018.

Barreras et al., 2026
Forests
~20%

Avocado expansion is linked to about 20% of Michoacán's deforestation, 2001–2017.

Cho et al., 2021
Conflict
2024

Two US inspectors were assaulted and briefly held in Michoacán. Avocado inspections there were suspended.

Associated Press, 2024

Production share: USDA Foreign Agricultural Service, GAIN Report MX2026-0019, "Avocado Annual," Guadalajara, March 12, 2026.
